Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up my HoloRes glasses?

Setting up your HoloRes glasses is simple. First, ensure your device is compatible with the specific 3D technology of your package. For Anaglyph glasses, simply put them on and play content through our streaming service. For polarized glasses, you'll need a compatible 3D display or projector. Autostereoscopic technology requires specialized displays that we can help you source.

What is the difference between the packages?

Our packages differ in the 3D technology used and the quality of experience:

  • Indie: Uses Anaglyph technology with color-filter glasses, perfect for home viewing.
  • Director: Enhanced Anaglyph with better color accuracy and moderate depth.
  • Studio: Professional polarized 3D for cinema-quality experiences.
  • HoloRes Premium: Cutting-edge glasses-free autostereoscopic technology.
How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription at any time through your PayPal account. Log in to PayPal, go to your automatic payments, find HoloRes, and cancel the subscription. If you need assistance, our support team can help guide you through the process.

What devices are compatible with HoloRes?

Compatibility varies by package:

  • Indie & Director: Compatible with all devices including smartphones, tablets, computers, and TVs.
  • Studio: Requires 3D-capable projectors or displays with polarized technology.
  • HoloRes Premium: Requires specialized autostereoscopic displays.
How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the US and 10-14 business days internationally. Express shipping (2-3 business days) is available for an additional fee. You'll receive a tracking number once your order ships.

Technical Support

Common Technical Issues

If you're experiencing issues with your HoloRes experience, try these troubleshooting steps first:

  • Poor 3D effect: Ensure you're using the correct glasses for your package and that they're properly calibrated
  • Blurry images: Check your display resolution settings and ensure content is playing at the recommended quality
  • No 3D effect: Verify that you're playing compatible 3D content through our streaming service
  • Connectivity issues: Restart your device and router, then try again

Advanced Troubleshooting

For more complex technical issues:

  • Update your device's graphics drivers
  • Check for firmware updates for your display/projector
  • Ensure your internet connection meets our minimum requirements (10 Mbps for HD, 25 Mbps for 4K)
  • Try using a different browser or device to isolate the issue

Setup & Installation Guide

Getting Started with Your HoloRes System

Follow these step-by-step instructions to set up your HoloRes experience based on your package:

1 Unbox Your Package

Carefully unbox your HoloRes package and ensure all components are present:

  • Hologlyph glasses (quantity varies by package)
  • Calibration image card
  • Encrypted QR code for account activation
  • User manual and setup guide

2 Activate Your Account

Scan the encrypted QR code using your smartphone or enter the activation code on our website to create your HoloRes account.

3 Calibrate Your Glasses

Follow the calibration process specific to your package:

  • Indie & Director: Display the calibration image on your screen and adjust the glasses until the image appears clear and properly aligned
  • Studio: Use the specialized calibration tool included with polarized glasses for optimal 3D effect
  • HoloRes Premium: No calibration needed for autostereoscopic displays

4 Set Up Your Display

Configure your display device according to your package requirements:

  • Indie & Director: Works with any standard display - no special configuration needed
  • Studio: Enable 3D mode on your compatible projector or display
  • HoloRes Premium: Connect your autostereoscopic display following manufacturer instructions

5 Access Content

Log in to your HoloRes account and start exploring our 3D content library. Download the HoloRes app for the best experience on mobile devices.

Returns & Refunds

Return Policy

We offer a 30-day return policy from the date of delivery. To be eligible for a return, your item must be in the same condition that you received it, unworn or unused, with tags, and in its original packaging.

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Log in to your HoloRes account and go to "Order History"
  2. Select the item(s) you wish to return
  3. Print the provided return label and shipping instructions
  4. Package your items securely and attach the return label
  5. Drop off at any authorized shipping location

Refunds

Once we receive your return, we will inspect it and notify you that we've received your returned item. We will immediately notify you of the status of your refund after inspecting the item.

If your return is approved, we will initiate a refund to your original payment method. You will receive the credit within 5-10 business days, depending on your card issuer's policies.

Exchanges

We only replace items if they are defective or damaged. If you need to exchange it for the same item, contact us at returns@holores.co.za.
