ABOUT

Our Mission

To develop, build, construct, and supply engineering equipment while maintaining all services in Southern Africa, improving the quality of living for all through integrated solutions and services.

Our Vision

To remain an industry leader in multi-disciplines within civil engineering, expanding our footprint across the construction economy in South Africa with excellence and innovation.

Who We Are

Founded in 2016 as a Black Owned enterprise, Partreds Pty (Ltd) has established itself as a trusted partner in engineering and construction. Led by Director Mzomhle Patwell Pindela, our team of professionals and artisans delivers quality and value through meticulous planning, attention to detail, and unwavering commitment to safety and compliance standards.

Our Services

Comprehensive engineering and construction solutions tailored to meet the demanding needs of the energy sector.

01

Engineering Equipment Supply

Comprehensive supply of pumps, valves, compressors, heat exchangers, and diesel tanks from industry-leading manufacturers including Sasol, Total, and BP.

02

Tank Installation

Professional installation of underground and aboveground tanks ranging from 9,000Lt to 83,000Lt with full compliance certification and safety documentation.

Fuel Filling Stations

Petroleum Transportation

Safe and reliable diesel, petroleum, and paraffin transportation services with full documentation, compliance, and bill of lading assurance.

05

Civil Construction

Road construction and civil engineering projects for residential, commercial, and government sectors with proven delivery excellence.

06

Project Management

Full project cycle management from scope of works to handover, ensuring timely delivery within budget and to exacting specifications.
